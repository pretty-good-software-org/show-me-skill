## [1.3.1] - 2026-11-09
### Changed
- The compatibility note says that terminal agents show fenced Mermaid as raw text unless a rendering hook draws it,
  instead of naming one hook.

## [1.3.0] - 2026-09-14
### Changed
- Say where fenced Mermaid renders, keep diagrams to the kinds terminal renderers draw (flowchart, sequence, state,
  class, and entity-relationship) and under 120 columns, and fall back to a call tree or pseudocode where Mermaid shows
  as raw text.

## [1.2.0] - 2026-09-10
### Added

## [1.1.0] - 2026-09-05
